# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and
constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give and accept constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect differing opinions and collaborate in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository
maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as
confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation when necessary.
